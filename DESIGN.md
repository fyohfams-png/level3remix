## Owner Direction & Overrides (R-30 / R-37 / R-19)
1. **Holywings (`holywings.com` / HW Group) Superclub Luxury Aesthetic (R-30 Explicit User Request)**:
   - Requested by the owner: *"buat tampilannya lebih mewah seperti web https://holywings.com/"*.
   - Captures the upscale Indonesian superclub & VIP nightlife atmosphere (The H Club / W Superclub / Golden Tiger): deep obsidian-velvet surfaces (`#050508`), warm brushed satin-champagne gold (`#d6b47c` / `#f0d7a7`), dramatic stage-beam lighting that bridges the sapphire-blue 3D L3 logo with warm champagne gold, and full-bleed **Superclub Headliner Poster** member cards.
2. **360° Bidirectional 3D Logo Rotation (R-19 / R-37 Explicit User Request)**:
   - Both the intro splash logo (`.intro-logo-img`) and hero emblem (`#heroLogoImg`) rotate 360° to the right and 360° back to the left (`spin360RightLeft`), paired with `#toggleLogoSpinBtn` pause control and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` support (R-39).

## Color Palette (2 Core Neutrals + 1 Satin Champagne Accent + Logo Sapphire Bridge)
- **Obsidian Velvet Base (`#050508`)**: Ultra-deep nightclub background (WCAG contrast 19.3:1 against `#faf7f2`).
- **VIP Lounge Charcoal Surface (`#0c0c12` / `#13131c`)**: Rich dark surface for the fixed VIP sidebar, editorial manifesto, studio audio rack, and city directory.
- **Brushed Satin Champagne Gold (`#d6b47c` / `#f0d7a7`)**: Refined, muted luxury champagne-gold (never harsh yellow) used for active VIP states, hairline frames, and primary CTAs (WCAG contrast 10.6:1 on `#0c0c12`, dark text `#050508` on `#d6b47c` = 10.9:1).
- **Warm Ivory (`#faf7f2`) & Champagne Silver (`#b8b2a6`)**: High-contrast editorial typography (18.4:1 and 9.4:1 on `#0c0c12`).

## Typography
- **Display & Headings (`Outfit`, 600-900)**: Bold uppercase superclub display headlines with sculpted tracking.
- **Body & VIP Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 400-700)**: Clean, legible typography for city filters, bios, and audio specs.
